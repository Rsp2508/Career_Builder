How resources are ranked

Every candidate gets a score between 0 and 1 from six weighted signals. Similarity compares text; skill gap measures how much of a resource addresses something missing; readiness checks whether its prerequisites are met; difficulty compares to your stated level; preference matches your interests; diversity penalises resources that overlap what is already on the list.

    Runs entirely in your browser. No API keys, no accounts, no network requests, nothing sent anywhere.

    Similarity is TF-IDF cosine over alias-expanded text, not neural sentence embeddings — a page with no network access cannot load a transformer model. It resolves synonyms such as NLP and natural language processing, but will not relate two phrases that share no vocabulary. Roles, skills and resources are a hand-authored prototype dataset.
